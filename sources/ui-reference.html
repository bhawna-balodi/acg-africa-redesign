How it works

From a rough idea to a packed bag in three steps

01

Tell us where and when

Pick a departure, or send a rough idea and a budget. A trip designer who has been there replies within one working day.

02

We plan it with you

You get a day-by-day route and one fixed price. Change anything you like. A 20 percent deposit holds your place.

03

Travel with a local leader

Meet your leader at the first hotel. Transport, tickets and tables are handled, and our team is on call day and night.
