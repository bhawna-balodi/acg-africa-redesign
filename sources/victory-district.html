Victory District™: from venue to ecosystem

Victory District™ transforms greenfield and brownfield sites, sports precincts, event venues and underutilised public assets into self-sustaining economic ecosystems. Each district is multi-use, with diversified revenue streams and activity across 365 days rather than matchdays alone.

An established model in mature markets, new to the African continent.

Districts are built to the standards of global sport’s governing bodies, so host nations are eligible to bid for and host international events.

Operations, maintenance and revenue are planned before construction begins, not after handover.

A stadium is a cost. A district is an economy.

Governments end up paying to keep empty stadiums standing. Investors will not back a single income line. The people next to the site want work that does not stop when the builders leave.

Victory District™ is a sport and entertainment district. Five things it has to do, on every site:

  • Fill the calendar: Conferences, concerts, markets and community use, not only the fixture list
  • Earn on ordinary days: Hospitality, retail, leisure and tenants who pay rent all year
  • Keep paying wages once the builders leave: Operations, security, maintenance, hospitality
  • Keep talent at home: Training and academy facilities on the site itself
  • Cover its own running costs: No recurring call on the public purse

The first district proves it. The pipeline is built by repeating it.

ACG is building a pipeline and new category to do that. Flagship project, then blueprint, then replication across Africa. Every project moves through the same gates: Concept, Feasibility, Bankability, Delivery, Operations.

Each one makes the next one faster to fund and easier to approve.

A scalable pipeline, not a single project. ACG is a long-term infrastructure origination engine.

No two districts are the same

Victory District™ is not a fixed master plan template. It is a co-creation framework: the model, mix of uses, governance and commercial structure are shaped through feasibility work with stakeholders. 

The method is the same everywhere.  The answer never is.

The Victory District™ Model

Victory District™ is built on three mutually reinforcing verticals, that have been embedded in the model from the outset. Together these verticals form the Victory District™ Model: providing the framework through which governments, DFIs and investors can assess its viability, impact and scalability.

1. Development

Integrated, mixed-use destinations, anchored by sports facilities and supported by a broad and diversified range of commercial and operational income streams.

2. Events and activation

Attracting major sporting competitions, international events, conferences, entertainment and a wide range of sectoral programmes to the districts.

3. Long-term operations

Professional, sustainable and commercially viable management of the districts, their venues and their supporting infrastructure.

Within these three verticals, every district is designed to:

  • Drive year-round utilisation
  • Expand revenue diversification beyond matchdays
  • Create jobs and youth opportunities
  • Support talent development pathways
  • Enable sustainable operations and maintenance
ACG is building a continental campaign to promote sport as a catalyst for economic development, investment and social impact.
