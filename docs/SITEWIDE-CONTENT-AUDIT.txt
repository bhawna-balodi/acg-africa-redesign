Sitewide content and component audit — 21 October 2026

Before implementation
Homepage: ambition -> need -> infrastructure failure -> mission. Preserve this
four-section story. The challenge's explanatory statement is incorrectly a large
accordion control; it should read as the introduction to the existing sequence.
Mission principles and closing action must stay together as a section conclusion.

Who We Are: provenance, delivery capacity, backing and method establish credibility;
the small category labels currently compete with the actual messages. Each statement
and its supporting evidence belong together. Explore Victory District and See what
we do are contextual links after their complete explanations. Introductory links
are page-level routes; distinguish their secondary role from the final contact action.
What ACG is / is not is a comparison, not a repeated stack of independent paragraphs.

What We Do: five gates explain commitment and accountability, followed by six work
steps explaining execution. Show these as two related but distinct process patterns.
The model CTA belongs after the complete venue-to-ecosystem explanation.

Victory District: problem -> operating requirements -> repeatability -> local
adaptation -> three reinforcing verticals -> outcomes -> stakeholder value.
Requirements are operating conditions, verticals are a model, and beneficiaries
are a value mapping. They should not all become interchangeable feature cards.
The contact action concludes the stakeholder section after every beneficiary.

Our Team: institutional purpose -> two leadership biographies -> independent board
-> academic panel. Names, roles and biographies need distinct type roles. Long
supporting career history can use explicit native disclosure; primary identity and
experience must remain visible. Portrait/name/role are one person, not separate
widgets. Profile links need consistent visible feedback. Section anchors must remain.

Profiles: individual identity -> role -> evidence/biography -> original reference
links. Original reference links belong after the associated biography, while the
board-return link is navigation rather than a primary conversion action.

News: dates -> headlines -> story. The latest item can lead the archive; article
pages require a narrow reading measure, a clear lead and semantic subheadings.
Back-to-news links are tertiary navigation. Original story/source links remain
within their narrative context; do not convert every link into a button.

Contact: original invitation and email precede one clear official-form action.
Keep the previously selected external form flow and state the destination honestly.

Shared corrections
- One type-role system across every route, with explicit display, page, section,
  item, lead, body and metadata roles; no label promoted above its main message.
- One container/grid, an 8px spacing rhythm, 8px image frames and shared controls.
- Filled actions indicate the final conversion. Secondary routes use outlines;
  contextual references use text links. Place actions after their owned content.
- Eliminate source-format empty paragraphs, trailing line-break gaps and accidental
  presentation markup while preserving every original word and punctuation mark.
- Use open lists, process rails, comparisons, directories and reading layouts
  according to content purpose. Avoid adding decorative icons or motion.

Aftermark validation scope

integrity, meaningful CTA destinations, responsiveness, keyboard controls and
reduced motion. Treat the document as quality control, not visual direction.
