Banner consistency audit — 22 October 2026

Observed drift: interior main offsets were fixed independently of the responsive
navbar; Contact Us had separate offsets and section-scale H1 styling; articles
used unrelated fixed image heights and logo-only frames had another height.

Resolution: src/styles/banners.css supplies shared entry/exit spacing, heading
weight/colour/tracking, lead widths, content gaps and image corner treatment.
It loads after page styles, including contact.css. Header clearance derives from
the actual shared navbar-height token, with desktop/tablet/mobile spacing modes.
The homepage retains its expressive headline and split composition. Editorial
articles retain a documented smaller title scale for their complete long titles.
Article images share a 4:3 frame; logo artwork uses contain rather than cover.
Corporate panoramic images share responsive heights and 8px corner treatment.
Contact retains its enlarged form, 4:3 image and functional side-by-side layout.
No source content, markup, imagery, links, handlers or motion was changed.

Validation: build and static verification passed on all 37 routes, including
official content coverage, semantic structure and email-draft behavior.
Browser page checks passed all 37 routes at 1440, 768 and 390px: no overflow,
broken images or duplicate/missing H1s. Banner-specific browser assertions pass
nine representative routes at 1440, 768, 390 and 320px: identical opening entry,
at least 32px navbar clearance, shared title styling and contained imagery.
Desktop corporate/contact and mobile article screenshots reviewed.
Reports: .qa/banner-results.json and .qa/pages-results.json.
