Themed contact form - 21 October 2026

User-selected delivery
- Prepare an email to info@acgafrica.com. This supersedes the previous choice
  to redirect to the official form. No backend, CAPTCHA simulation or fake
  delivery confirmation is used. Submit opens the user email editor; sending
  the draft is a separate user action. Input values are never reset.

Source and placement
- Original Get in Touch heading, description, email, six required field labels,
  all country-code options, South Africa default, required legend and Submit
  label are extracted from sources/contact-us.html. No new business fields.
- One reusable component appears on Contact Us and the existing closing contact
  sections on Who We Are and Our Team. Homepage/nav CTAs lead to local contact.
- Shared type, spacing, colors, borders and CTA styles; desktop editorial split
  and a stacked mobile form. Existing images remain in stable inline frames.

Behavior and accessibility
- Native required/email validation and text whitespace validation; visible labels,
  autocomplete, phone input mode, inline errors and an announced status region.
- Copy enquiry prepares the same content. If clipboard access fails, a focused
  selectable text area provides a manual fallback. This does not send anything.
- No-JavaScript mode retains the native mailto form and validation; JS-only Copy
  control remains hidden. No entered details are saved in browser storage.

Implementation
- src/templates/contact-form.mjs: shared themed form rendering.
- src/content/contact-form.json: extracted official schema.
- scripts/import-pages.mjs: reproducible extraction and three placements.
- src/styles/contact.css: scoped responsive contact layout.
- src/scripts/contact.js: validation, draft encoding, clipboard/manual fallback.

  Submit handler tested with an isolated email editor: correct recipient, all

- Three pages pass layout/controls at 1440/1024/768/390/320px (15 checks).
- Browser tests pass empty/invalid email validation, successful copy, rejected
